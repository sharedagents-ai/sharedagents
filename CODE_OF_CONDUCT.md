# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

By participating, you agree to uphold a welcoming, harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussion, review, and disagreement.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may ban
contributors for sustained or severe violations.

Report incidents privately to **admin@sharedagents.ai**. All reports are reviewed
and handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
